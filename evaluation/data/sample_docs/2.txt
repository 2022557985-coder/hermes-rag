The Solar System has eight planets. Mercury is the closest to the Sun, with a diameter of 4,879 km and an orbital period of 88 Earth days. Venus has a surface temperature of 462°C, making it the hottest planet, even though it is farther from the Sun than Mercury. Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon, and a surface gravity of 9.807 m/s². Mars is known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide on its surface, and it has two moons: Phobos and Deimos. Jupiter is the largest planet, with a mass 318 times that of Earth and at least 95 known moons as of 2023. Saturn is famous for its ring system, which is composed primarily of ice particles. Uranus rotates on its side with an axial tilt of 97.77 degrees. Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun, discovered through mathematical prediction in 1846.
